Local team wins championship title

The inaugural season of Red Bluff United FC’s U12 Boys team ended with a statement—a championship victory and a historic first for Red Bluff boys soccer. Over Memorial Day weekend, United FC captured the Rogue Memorial Challenge championship in Medford, Oregon, finishing the tournament undefeated and showcasing the determination and composure that have defined the club’s first competitive season. The title is believed to be the first Rogue Memorial Challenge championship won by a Red Bluff-area boys soccer team, making the accomplishment a historic milestone for the local soccer community.
